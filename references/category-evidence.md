# Category evidence hints

Use these notes only to decide which real screenshots are useful. They do not define the write-up's structure, tone, or wording.

## Web

Look for the decisive application evidence: an unusual route such as `/robots.txt`, a hidden endpoint, source or JavaScript clue, request/response change, cookie/header/token change, an actually used decoder, or the response containing the flag.

## Crypto

Look for the actual ciphertext or input, the relevant property or clue the participant noticed, the exact transform/tool/script they used, and the decoded result.

## Pwn

Look for the target service or binary, security information only if it mattered, the actual payload or exploit execution, and the shell or flag output.

## Reverse

Look for the function, string, disassembly, or control-flow clue that mattered, the recovered logic/key/constant, the participant's actual script or tool output, and the successful result.

## Forensics

Look for the original artifact, decisive metadata/string/carving/packet/file discovery, the extraction or decoding action, and the recovered result.

## OSINT

Look for the original image or text clue, the decisive corroborating source or map/street-view evidence, and the exact location/name/coordinates used in the answer.

## Misc

Show the clue, the actual manipulation or discovery, and the proof of success.

Do not add theory or claims that are not in the participant's own account.
